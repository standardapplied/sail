- **Every page of the app is drawn in one skeleton.** A project's page, This Mac's page and Account share one column, at most 720 pt wide and centred in the window, with the header and the content in it. In a wide window, This Mac's header no longer sits off to the right while its list starts at the left.
- **This Mac reads as a page like any other.** The title is "This Mac", the Mac's name sits under it, and New Shell is at the top right where a project's page puts New Workspace. With no shell open, the page shows the project page's empty state, "No shells open", with New Shell. The LOCAL badge in the left panel, the LOCAL · NOT SYNCED chip, the sentence about what is sent to the control plane and the paragraph about what This Mac does not have are gone. The foot's keys read as a project's do: Open, New, End Session.
- **Account says less.** The header is your name with your email under it, with no handle or role. Passkeys say only "A new passkey needs a ticket from your admin." and Keys say only "Agents sign in with their own account or a key stored here." Sign Out stands alone, without the two lines about this Mac and the runner. The control plane row shows a pill only while it is not connected, and this Mac's passkey says "This Mac" in its detail line rather than in a tag. The user menu no longer has a Keys item.
