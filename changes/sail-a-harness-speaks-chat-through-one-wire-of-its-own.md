- **A chat whose agent stops reading no longer hangs.** What Sail writes to a chat's agent (your message, an answer, Stop, a change of mode) now goes out in order on a writer of its own, so an agent that stops reading holds up nothing else: sending, answering, Stop and ending the chat each still answer at once. A line the agent has not taken five minutes after Sail began writing it ends its process, and the turn is cut in the words a chat already says ("…did not get your answer: its process stopped taking what Sail wrote to it, so Sail ended it; your next message starts it again."). If Sail cannot record what it wrote to the agent, it restarts the agent and sends your message again, and the chat says so. Everything else a chat does is unchanged, byte for byte.
