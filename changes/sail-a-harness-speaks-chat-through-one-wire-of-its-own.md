- **A chat whose agent stops reading no longer hangs.** What Sail writes to a chat's agent (your message, an answer, Stop, a change of mode) now goes out in order on a writer of its own, so an agent that stops reading holds up nothing else: sending, answering, Stop and ending the chat each still answer at once. A line the agent has not taken five minutes after it was sent ends its process, and the turn is cut in the words a chat already says ("…did not get your answer: its process stopped taking what Sail wrote to it, so Sail ended it; your next message starts it again."). Everything else a chat does is unchanged, byte for byte.
