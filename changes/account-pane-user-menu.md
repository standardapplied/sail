- **The Account pane speaks the user menu's language.** Signed in: the person's name as the display heading, `@handle · Role` under a SIGNED IN AS eyebrow, the control plane as the menu's own line under CONTROL PLANE (its address, a state square and word, live as a check starts and ends: Connected, Reconnecting, Can’t reach), the session's end in quiet text, and Sign Out as the secondary button. Signed out: "Sign in to Sail", the control plane's field and the ticket's under their eyebrows, Sign In with Passkey the one primary and Enroll with a Ticket secondary. A refusal is said under the field it is about, the field drawn refused (a crimson edge until it is edited again) and the sentence after the error's mark in crimson, the rest in the secondary ink, never a tinted row. A long name or handle is cut at its end and said in full on hover; the session's end wraps. The menu and the pane draw the control plane with one view and say who and where from one source, so they cannot disagree.
- **The closed user menu is no element to VoiceOver.** The key catcher is the menu, with its items as children, only while it is open.
- **Real clicks stay in the drive.** The user menu's click-outside test calls the menu's own handler, a test helper hands the view its own handler for every mouse button, and a sweep fails any app unit test that sends a mouse event through `sendEvent`, the app's or a window's. The menu's tests hold nothing that is the whole process's: each shell's menu is told its window gave up the keys by a notice of the test's own, and its Appearance goes through a recorder, so a plain local `swift test` passes beside every other suite. A sweep fails any view of the app that changes, in a layout pass (`layout()`, `updateConstraints`, `viewDidLayout`, `resizeSubviews`, `setFrameSize`), what forces another pass, which once crashed the drive; and the tests and the drive now write any exception AppKit reports or ends the app on, with its reason and stack.
