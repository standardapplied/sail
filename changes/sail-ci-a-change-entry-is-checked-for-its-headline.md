- **A change entry's every bullet opens with its bold headline.** `ci/changelog check` and the release's fold (`ci/changelog X.Y.Z`) refuse a bullet that does not open as `- **…** …`, with the headline closed on its own line. The refusal names the file and line and shows that shape, and `changes/README.md` quotes it. A bullet marked `*` or `+` is refused too. A line that is not a bullet still continues the bullet above it. Before this, only the `- ` that opens an entry was checked, so sail#547's first entry passed without a headline.
