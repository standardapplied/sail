- **A process the app or `sail-term-probe` starts holds none of their connections.** The local terminal host, and any child a test starts, gets its three standard descriptors and nothing else: `POSIX_SPAWN_CLOEXEC_DEFAULT` on macOS, `posix_spawn_file_actions_addclosefrom_np` on Linux (glibc 2.34 or later), from one place, `Spawn.process`. Every socket and pipe SailKit makes is close-on-exec, from one place, `Descriptors`. A connection the client closes is closed only while no process is starting, because a process being started holds a copy of every descriptor until its exec. Before, a child kept a copy of whatever was open when it started, so a port the app had stopped listening on could still take a connection, and a connection the app had closed stayed open. That is what turned main's SailKit lanes red at e15edf410: a test's child held another test's stopped listener, which took a reconnect the test expected to be refused. The test fakes now accept only on a listener they still hold, never on a number already closed and given to another socket. `TogetherTests` runs those two tests together 20 times in one process, in every SailKit lane's own run.
