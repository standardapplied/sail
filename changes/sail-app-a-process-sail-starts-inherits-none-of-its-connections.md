- **A process the app or `sail-term-probe` starts holds none of their connections.** The local terminal host starts with its three standard descriptors and nothing else of the process that started it, and every socket and pipe the client makes is close-on-exec. Before, the host, which outlives the app, kept copies of what the app had open when it started it, the app's terminal connections among them, so a connection the app closed stayed open at the other end until the host ended. The host `sail-term-probe --local` starts now has every signal at its default action and none blocked, and reads `/dev/null` as its standard input instead of the probe's; one that ends before it says it serves is reported as having ended, no longer as not serving within 20 s.
