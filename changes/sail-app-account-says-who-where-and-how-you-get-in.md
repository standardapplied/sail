- **The Mac app's Account view says who you are, where, and how you get in.** A header: the initial's filled disc, the name, `@handle`, the role as a plain pill and the email, the name, handle and role being the user menu's own. Then four groups. The control plane: its address, "Signed in until … · N days left", and its state as a pill (Connected, Reconnecting pulsing, Can’t reach in the error's ink); out of reach, a banner in the group says so with Try Again; under three days left the end turns to the warning's ink and Sign In Again runs the sign-in ceremony, stays in the view and asks for the groups again, also when the ceremony is refused; a session already past its end says it ended, with Sign In Again. Your passkeys, from `GET /v1/passkeys`: a row each, "This Mac" on the one this session signed in with, when it was added and last used. Your keys: a row for each harness `GET /v1/agents` lists, in its order, with its default key ("API key “default” · new sessions and spec runs use it") and Replace, or "No key." ("No key in use." when it has others) and how its sessions sign in without one, with Add Key; a further key of a harness is a row of its own, "not in use", offering Remove alone. Then Sign Out with its icon, beside what signing out does and does not end. No button in the view is primary. The groups are asked at once and each is drawn as its answer lands; only the latest ask's answers are kept, an ask under way stops when the view is left, and nothing is asked while it is not shown. Each list shows Sail's drawing mark until its first answer, keeps its last answer, dimmed, while the call that drew it fails, and says the failure under it; a call that finds the session ended is not said in its group: it hands over at once to the check, which signs out as every check does, even when the view is left meanwhile.
- **Keys and passkeys are removed from the Account view, asking first.** ⋯ ▸ Remove asks in a sheet whose default is Cancel: a key's removal says that running sessions keep it and what new sessions and spec runs do without it, in the harness's own words, and sends `DELETE /v1/logins/{harness}/{name}`; a passkey's says it stops signing you in and signs out wherever it is signed in, and sends `DELETE /v1/passkeys/{id}`, its `204` read as done with nothing parsed. A removal under way says "Removing" on its row, which takes no second Remove. The server's refusal (the last passkey, the one this session signed in with, a session no passkey signed in) is said under that row in the server's words, the row kept, until the groups are asked again; one that comes while the view is hidden is said once it is shown, and a removal that finds the session ended says nothing under its row and hands over to the check at once. Add Key and Replace open the Store a Key sheet, which is now built from a harness's declaration, so the Account view and the New Session panel open the same sheet. Replace is offered only on the key in use and stores under its name; renamed with the sheet's Change, the new key becomes the one in use. A key added where none is in use becomes the one new sessions take, and New Session starts with it, as when it is stored from the New Session panel.
- **The user menu's Keys opens the Account view at its keys.** It is no longer disabled, and its "later version" sentence is gone.
- **The client goldens record a login removed, and an answer with no body records none.** `login-removed` is new; `passkey-removed` keeps no `"body"`; the passkey refusals are named by their codes (`refusal-conflict-passkey-current`, `refusal-conflict-passkey-last`, `refusal-forbidden-passkey-no-passkey-session`, `refusal-not-found-passkey-not-yours`).
