- **The Mac app's Account view says who you are, where, and how you get in.** A header: the initial's disc, the name, `@handle`, the role as a plain pill and the email, the name, handle and role being the user menu's own. Then four groups. The control plane: its address, "Signed in until … · N days left", and its state as a pill (Connected, Reconnecting pulsing, Can’t reach in the error's ink); out of reach, a banner in the group says so with Try Again; under three days left the end turns to the warning's ink and Sign In Again runs the sign-in ceremony and stays in the view. Your passkeys, from `GET /v1/passkeys`: a row each, "This Mac" on the one this session signed in with, when it was added and last used. Your keys: a row for each harness `GET /v1/agents` lists, in its order, with its default key ("API key “default” · new sessions and spec runs use it") and Replace, or "No key." and how its sessions sign in without one, with Add Key; a further key of a harness is a row of its own. Then Sign Out with its icon, beside what signing out does and does not end. No button in the view is primary. Each list shows Sail's drawing mark until its first answer, keeps its last answer, dimmed, while the call that drew it fails, and says the failure under it.
- **Keys and passkeys are removed from the Account view, asking first.** ⋯ ▸ Remove asks in a sheet whose default is Cancel: a key's removal says that running sessions keep it and what new sessions and spec runs do without it, in the harness's own words, and sends `DELETE /v1/logins/{harness}/{name}`; a passkey's says it stops signing you in and signs out wherever it is signed in, and sends `DELETE /v1/passkeys/{id}`, its `204` read as done with nothing parsed. The server's refusal (the last passkey, the one this session signed in with, a session no passkey signed in) is said under that row in the server's words, the row kept. Add Key and Replace open the Store a Key sheet, which is now built from a harness's declaration, so the Account view and the New Session panel open the same sheet; a key stored beside another is made the one new sessions take.
- **The user menu's Keys opens the Account view at its keys.** It is no longer disabled, and its "later version" sentence is gone.
- **The client goldens record a login removed, and an answer with no body records none.** `login-removed` is new; `passkey-removed` keeps no `"body"`; the passkey refusals are named by their codes (`refusal-conflict-passkey-current`, `refusal-conflict-passkey-last`, `refusal-forbidden-passkey-no-passkey-session`, `refusal-not-found-passkey-not-yours`).
