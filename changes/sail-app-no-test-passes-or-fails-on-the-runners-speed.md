- **The SailKit macOS lane says why its tests stalled.** It prints the runner image it ran on, keeps the suites' event stream in its `sailkit-macos-reports` artifact, and runs the suites under `scripts/stalls`, which ticks the machine's load every second and, when no test has reported for 10 s, keeps the processes using the CPU, a `sample` of every test process and, once, a `spindump` of the machine beside them.
- **No terminal or files socket test fails because its process stalled.** A test's waits on a fake host, a session's follower, a held wait or a timer are waits for that event, ended only by the suite's time limit, which every socket suite now has; the fake host reads what a client sends on a thread of its own, so a client's write never waits for the test to be scheduled. A client limit a test is not about is `Patience.answer`, 120 s, past every suite's limit. A terminal session reads how long its attempts, attachments and unused tickets took from a `SessionClock` (the continuous clock, unless a test hands its own), and the probe's `Runner` takes its waits' patience and its close's limit, 5 s and 10 s as before.
