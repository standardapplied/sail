- **This Mac is a project in Workspaces, listed first, kept on this Mac alone.** The Mac app's projects list has two groups: THIS MAC, holding one project named This Mac with the terminal icon and a Local pill, then the control plane's projects under its host with their count. This Mac's page says which Mac it is and that nothing on it is sent to the control plane, and lists this Mac's terminals, a row each: the tab's title, its folder, Running or Ended, and the shell or how its session ended. Open (Return) shows that terminal in Terminals; End Session (⌘W) ends it as closing it does, asking by its name; New Shell (⌘T) opens a shell in Terminals at once, with no chooser. This Mac cannot be renamed or deleted and has no repositories, agents, size or template; no request names it or this Mac's host. Chosen last, it is where Workspaces opens next time.
- **This Mac needs nothing from the control plane.** Signed in, This Mac is listed and its page shows while the projects load and when the control plane cannot be reached: the control plane's group then says it cannot be reached, with Try Again, and the card saying why stands beside the list. Choosing This Mac lists the terminals a person had before quitting and starts no shell of its own.
- **Untitled shells on this Mac are told apart.** A second untitled shell's tab is "This Mac 2", as a second tab in one workspace is "harbor · dock 2", and a tab keeps its number while it is open: closing or moving another tab renames none. A tab the person named shows that name, and one whose program titled it shows that title.
