Sail then checks who is signed in once, however many calls found it together and however long ago they started, and takes you to the welcome, which says when your sign-in ended, from whichever section you were in. Signed in again as the same person, your runner tabs come back attached, files panel and all. A call that fails for any other reason says what it said before.
