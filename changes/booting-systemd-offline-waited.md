- **A template whose systemd answers `offline` while it boots is waited for, not failed.** A booting systemd answers `systemctl is-system-running` with `offline` until it has made `/run/systemd/system`, and the build's boot wait took that for a final state, failing the build ("its boot did not settle: systemd says it is offline"). `offline` is now waited out within the bound while PID 1 is systemd, and said at once only when PID 1 is something else ("its PID 1 is sleep, not systemd, so it has no boot to wait for"); every other state a wait can turn into `running` is waited out too, and only `maintenance` and `stopping` end the wait at once.
