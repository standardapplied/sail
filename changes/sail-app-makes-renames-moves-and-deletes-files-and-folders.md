- **The app makes, renames, moves and deletes a workspace's files and folders.** In the files tree a ⋯ on the row under the pointer and on the chosen row, the row's context menu and the File menu hold New File ⌥⌘N, New Folder ⇧⌘N, Rename F2, Reveal in Terminal and Delete ⌘⌫ (in the error ink); nothing stands on a row at rest. New and Rename are a name field in the row's own place: ⏎ sends the one create or move, Esc closes it having made nothing, and a refusal is its sentence under the field, which stays open with the name chosen ("notes.md is already there."). Nothing is ever replaced, and no row is guessed: what was made or moved to appears, and is chosen, once the workspace's watch lists it. Rows are chosen several at once with ⌘ and ⇧, and dragged into another folder, each by one move; a folder dropped into itself or its child is refused before anything is sent ("A folder cannot be moved into itself."). Delete asks once, its button in `error` and Cancel the default, naming the item ("Delete src and everything in it? This cannot be undone.") or counting several, and saying when an open file's unsaved text is among them, which stays in its pane; "Some of src could not be deleted." when part of a folder stays. Reveal in Terminal pastes the shell-quoted path at the cursor of the tab's terminal through its own paste (bracketed where the program asked, the paste question for a path holding a newline), with no line break, so nothing runs; with no terminal in the tab it is off. A file open in the pane follows its rename or move, read at the new name, its unsaved text kept. The empty tree says "~/workspace is empty. ⌥⌘N makes a file."
- **The file pane takes only the answers to its own requests.** With a file open, a folder's listing refused, and every create, move or remove answered, reach the tree again rather than the pane.
