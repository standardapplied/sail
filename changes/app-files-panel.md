- **A workspace's tab shows its files, live.** ⌃⌘S (View ▸ Show Files) opens a 236 pt panel at the tab's leading edge: a plain tree of `/home/dev/workspace`, rows only, no header, title or count. Rows are 24 pt, folders first and sorted as Finder sorts, an 11 pt triangle on folders only, the chosen row semibold with the accent bar. Folders open lazily with →, Space or a double-click; ← shuts one or goes up. The tree follows the workspace through the runner's watch: a change in a shown folder lists it again whole, keeps the selection and the scroll, and marks an entry that appeared with a small teal square for 10 s (never one of a first listing). The panel speaks in one quiet line at its top only when something is not normal, in the reason the workspace gave where it gave one: "Listing ~/workspace" after 300 ms, "Still listing ~/workspace (6 s). The terminal still works.", "Not live since 14:02." with the tree dimmed, adding "Reconnecting." only while it truly is (a connection silent for 4 s is closed and tried again, so a slept laptop recovers in seconds), "Can't reach dock's files." with Try Again, a stopped workspace's own record with Start (looking never starts a workspace) over a dimmed tree, one connection over the runner's cap in its words with Try Again, "~/workspace is empty.", and past 1,024 open folders "Following the 1,024 most recent folders live.". A folder the workspace refuses says so under its own row; a broken link says where it leads when opened. In a tab too narrow for the terminals beside it the panel floats over them; its being shown is remembered per workspace. The files connection rides out a drop on the same reconnect waits as the tab's terminals, now one value both use. Names are held by their bytes, so a name that is not UTF-8 shows with replacement characters and is never confused with one that reads alike; VoiceOver hears the tree as an outline, each row's depth and whether it is open. The connection runs only while the files are on screen: not with the window closed, in the Dock or the app hidden (⌘H). A tree is live once the host speaks after a connection is admitted, a tick or a listing, not at admission alone, so a host whose watch has stalled never shows the tree live. A folder that changed while it was listed is asked again at the next tick, so one that never stops changing (an expanded `node_modules` under `npm install`) is listed once a tick and holds back nothing else: other folders' changes arrive and a folder opened meanwhile is watched. Named again by the host meanwhile, it still waits for the tick, and after three listings refused in a row the line says so: "node_modules keeps changing; shown as of 14:02." ⌃⌘S is one View item for what is in front: Show or Hide Files in a workspace's tab, Show or Hide Sidebar on the Workspaces page, off with its reason elsewhere.
