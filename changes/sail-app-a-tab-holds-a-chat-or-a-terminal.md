- **A tab can hold a chat, a new chat or a terminal (SailKit; nothing is drawn yet).** The sessions listing is now read row by row by its `form`. Chats are read with their state and title, and a row with no form fails the listing. A tab holds a chat, a new chat or a terminal. A new chat can change its workspace and harness until its first message binds it to its chat. ⌘T opens a tab like the one in front, and a person who starts in Chat with no tab gets a new chat in their last workspace. A split of a chat opens a terminal in its workspace. A person's live sessions that no tab holds are adopted as last tabs, oldest first. Each tab has one mark: needs you, error, an unseen turn, working or ended. The saved tab layout keeps chats, new chats, unsent drafts and the last workspace. Its version is bumped, so tab layouts saved by earlier builds are dropped once.
