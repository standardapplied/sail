- **A person can hold a chat with Claude Code in their own workspace, and the server logs every item of it.** `POST /v1/projects/{p}/workspaces/{w}/chats {harness, mode, inside}` opens one (`201 {session}`), admitted and refused exactly as a pty agent start is, word for word, and only for a harness with a chat form and in one of its chat modes; nothing starts until a message. `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/chat/messages {text}` logs the message and hands it to the one process that holds the conversation, started (signed in as a terminal's agent is, launched as an argv, never through a shell) or resumed by the harness's own id in the mode it last said when none does; said while a turn is open it is held in the log and answered `queued`, then handed when the turn ends. A message is text only in this release. `GET /v1/sessions/{id}/chat?after=<seq>` answers the items after `seq` at once, or holds up to 25 seconds for one; `POST /v1/sessions/{id}:end` ends the process and the chat, whose log stays readable for 30 days. Every route is the opener's alone, decided as entering the workspace is; any other caller is told there is no such chat.
- **What the agent does on its own is said truthfully.** Its text is gathered over 50 ms into one item a block; a turn it begins itself, a retry, a compaction, an interrupted or failed turn each have their item. A tool row left open when the turn ends is closed as unread. A request is logged and held; nothing answers it yet, so after 5 minutes Sail ends the turn and says why. A request it cannot read whole, or one too large to show a person whole, is refused at once (the agent told to split a large one), or, naming nothing to answer, its turn is stopped. When the process ends, sail stops or sail restarts, an open turn is cut and said so, its rows closed and its requests withdrawn, and the next message resumes it. An idle process ends after the project's `max_idle`.
- **The chat log is the person's and the box's.** `chat_sessions` and `chat_items` are box-local and never synced: removing a person, deleting a workspace (by the API or the command line), destroying a project or erasing it everywhere erases its chats. The schema is v1007 (a fleet reinstall).
- **One way to end a process over pipes.** The live room member and the chat share `ProcessEnd`: stdin closed, then the INT and TERM grace (`ProcessEnd.Grace`, moved from `LiveMembers`), and the wait between them. A pipe process's wrapper makes the folder its pid file is in.
