- **A person can hold a chat with Claude Code in their own workspace, and the server logs every item of it.** `POST /v1/projects/{p}/workspaces/{w}/chats {harness, mode, inside}` opens one (`201 {session}`), admitted and refused exactly as a pty agent start is, word for word, and only for a harness with a chat form and in one of its chat modes; nothing starts until a message. `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/chat/messages {text}` logs the message and hands it to the one process that holds the conversation, started (signed in as a terminal's agent is, launched as an argv, never through a shell) or resumed by the harness's own id in the mode it last said when none does; said while a turn is open, or while the process ends, it is held in the log and answered `queued`, then handed when the turn ends or by the next process. A message is text only in this release. `GET /v1/sessions/{id}/chat?after=<seq>` answers the items after `seq` at once, or holds up to 25 seconds for one, and says `ended` only with an ended chat's last item; `POST /v1/sessions/{id}:end` ends the process and the chat, whose log stays readable for 30 days. Every route is the opener's alone, decided as entering the workspace is; any other caller is told there is no such chat.
- **One conversation, one process, across restarts.** A chat's every process is found by one pid file in the chat's own folder. Before any start, and when sail starts (before it serves a request), a process left over is signalled and proved gone, and none starts beside it until it is (`409 agent_already_running` meanwhile); a process whose reading stops for any reason is ended, and takes no message from then on. A chat ended while a process starts for it has that process ended. Messages left waiting when a process ends or sail restarts are delivered by the next process, started as the chat's opener (after a restart, once sail serves); one that may not start now is said in the refusal's words and its messages keep waiting.
- **What the agent does on its own is said truthfully.** Its text is gathered over 50 ms into one item a block; a turn it begins itself, a retry, a compaction, an interrupted or failed turn each have their item. A tool row or a request left open when the turn ends is closed as unread or withdrawn. A request is logged and held; nothing answers it yet, so after 5 minutes Sail stops the turn and says why, and ends the process only if the turn does not stop. A request it cannot read whole, or one too large to show a person whole, is refused at once (the agent told to split a large one), or, naming nothing to answer, its turn is stopped. When the process ends, sail stops or sail restarts, an open turn is cut once and said so, its rows closed and its requests withdrawn, and the next message resumes it. An idle process ends after the project's `max_idle`, never cutting a message or a turn it begins itself.
- **The chat log is the person's and the box's.** `chat_sessions` and `chat_items` are box-local and never synced: removing a person (and their running process), deleting a workspace (by the API or the command line), destroying a project (whether or not its container was left) or erasing it everywhere erases its chats. The schema is v1007 (a fleet reinstall).
- **One way to end a process over pipes.** The live room member and the chat share `ProcessEnd`: stdin closed, then the INT and TERM grace (`ProcessEnd.Grace`, moved from `LiveMembers`), the wait between them, and the halt of one left over from before a restart (`ProcessEnd.halted`). A pipe process's wrapper makes the folder its pid file is in.
- **A process left over is signalled only while it is the one sail started.** A pipe process's wrapper records the process's start beside its pid (`agent.pid.start`), and the halt of one left over compares it with the process now at that pid before any signal (`AgentSession.killStarted`, by the same start-time rule a run's stop uses). After a restart of the box or the workspace, a pid the box has given another of the person's processes is never signalled: the process the file was written for is gone, and the file is cleaned. This closes the same hole for the live room member, whose leftover halt is the same helper.
