- **Your workspaces sit under their projects in the Workspaces panel.** Each project row has a disclosure; open, it lists your own workspaces in it, 14 pt in, each with its state square in the colours and states its page's row uses, then one quiet row "Other people's · N" that shows the project's page. Choosing a workspace shows its project's page with that row chosen. → opens a project or goes to its first workspace, ← shuts it or goes back to the project, and Return opens a workspace as its page's row does. A long workspace name yields in its middle and says itself whole on hover.
- **New Session from a workspace's row in the panel.** A workspace row's + opens the New Session popover beside the panel, its top on the row's where the window has room under it, else as high as it needs, always inside the window; the + stays pressed while the popover is open. One + shows at a time, the row's under the pointer, else the chosen row's, and only where the row's page offers New Session (your own running or stopped workspace, which a start there starts, as Start and Open does).
- **The projects you leave open stay open.** Which projects are open is kept on this Mac for each person and control plane, and never sent anywhere; the project you chose last opens at launch. Opening a project asks for its workspaces once, ⌘R and coming back to Workspaces ask again for every open one, and the panel and the project's page show the one answer. A stop or delete that finishes while you are in another project shows under its project at once, and a workspace created there as soon as its project is listed again. A project whose workspaces could not be listed says so under it, and the other projects stay as they are.
