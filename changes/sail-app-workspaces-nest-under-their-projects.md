- **Your workspaces sit under their projects in the Workspaces panel.** Each project row has a disclosure; open, it lists your own workspaces in it, 14 pt in, each with its state square in the colours and states its page's row uses, then one quiet row "Other people's · N" that shows the project's page. Choosing a workspace shows its project's page with that row chosen. → opens a project or goes to its first workspace, ← shuts it or goes back to the project, and Return opens a workspace as its page's row does. A long workspace name yields in its middle and says itself whole on hover.
- **New Session from a workspace's row in the panel.** A workspace row's + (shown while the row is chosen or under the pointer) opens the New Session popover beside the panel, its top on the row's, always inside the window.
- **The projects you leave open stay open.** Which projects are open is kept on this Mac for each person and control plane, and never sent anywhere; the project you chose last opens at launch. Opening a project asks for its workspaces once, ⌘R and coming back to Workspaces ask again for every open one, and the panel and the project's page show the one answer. A project whose workspaces could not be listed says so under it, and the other projects stay as they are.
