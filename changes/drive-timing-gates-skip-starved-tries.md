- **The drive no longer fails a timing gate on a starved runner, and shows drift.** A step whose figures are taken over tries (`f-budgets`, `p-figures`) reads the machine's CPUs once over all its tries' spans; when other processes left the app under 5% of at least 100 ticks (the idle ticks and the app's own, so the app's own extra work never excuses itself), its gates are judged at twice their ceilings, so a gross regression still fails, and the step's line, a CI warning and the job summary say "not measured cleanly: machine starved". A shorter reading, or one the sampler's window cut, is said and the step judged at its ceilings; the files flood's change p95, whose tries are a few milliseconds, always reads too short. Single long waits keep their own rule (`MachineShare`), with the same reading of the app's share. Ceilings that sat inside the runner's own range move out of it, the range written beside them: `p-figures`' costliest load turn and a key to its glyph gate at 32 ms (they read 9.4 to 17.6 ms and 7.2 to 16.7 ms, and 16 ms failed main itself), with tests that a key never copies the pane's text and a slice is laid out once; `f-budgets`' two scroll figures at 48 ms (they read 10.8 to 35.6 ms and 10.6 to 32.2 ms); the old ceilings are reported as marks. Every run writes `figures.tsv` (each figure, its budget, mark and ceiling, the machine's idle and our share) into the drive's output and its job summary, and `clients/SailApp/ci/figures-trend` prints main's last 30 runs as one series a figure.
