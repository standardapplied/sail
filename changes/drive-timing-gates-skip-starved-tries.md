- **The drive no longer fails a timing gate on a starved runner.** A figure taken over tries (`f-budgets`, `p-figures`, the files flood's change p95) does not count a try during which the machine's CPUs read 0% idle; the step takes more tries, up to twice its count, until enough count, and judges the first that count at the ceiling as before. A try whose span the sampler read fewer than twice counts, and the line says so. If none counts, every try is judged at twice the ceiling, so a gross regression still fails, and the step's line and the run's summary say "not measured cleanly". Extra tries are capped at 120 s for the whole drive. Single long waits keep their own rule (`MachineShare`). `p-figures`' two CPU figures (the costliest load turn, a key to its glyph) gate at 32 ms, two 60 Hz frames: on hosted runners they read 9.4 to 17.6 ms and 7.2 to 16.7 ms, and the 16 ms ceiling failed main itself; the 16 and 8 ms marks and the p95 are reported.
