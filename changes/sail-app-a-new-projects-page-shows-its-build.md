- **A new project's page shows its template building, step by step.** In the Mac app, a project whose template is being built, or whose last build failed, and that has no workspace yet shows a build card in place of the old "template is being built" page: Sail's mark drawing itself, "Creating harbor", who started the build and when, and the time since; a bar filled by the control plane's step of its steps with "step 10 of 20"; then "Project saved" and five groups (Container created, Tools installed, Repositories cloned, Agents installed, Ready for workspaces), each done, running or to come. The groups are found by the names of the control plane's steps, so a step this app does not know joins the group before it. The page reads the template every 2 s while the build runs, one read at a time, and stops when you leave the page, choose another project, sign out, close the window or the build ends; a read with no answer keeps the card and says so quietly, and asks again. A read the control plane refuses stops the reads and says its words on the card; a project that is gone is listed away, and an ended session is said on the welcome. Once built, the card gives way to the project's page and New Workspace is offered at once.
- **A failed or refused build says why, with Build Again.** A build that stopped says at which step, marks the group that failed, and gives the control plane's reason and what to do in words, never a command; Build Again, for those whose sign-in may build, picks up where it stopped and is asked once however often it is pressed. Others see the same card, "Started by ana at 14:02", told to ask an admin to build it again. A refused build is said on the card instead of over the page; one that got no answer reads the template, and the card says what the control plane holds: building, failed, or, only when none started, that it did not start.
